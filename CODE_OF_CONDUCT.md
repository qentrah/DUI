# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when needed, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and respect project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained hostile communication
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the qentrah maintainers through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where possible.
